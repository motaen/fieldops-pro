Construction Operations. Connected.

FieldOps Pro is a construction operations platform designed to bring critical field workflows into one connected environment — helping project teams improve visibility, coordination, compliance and decision-making.

From field activity to management insight — in one platform.

The Problem

Large construction projects rely on multiple contractors, consultants, documents, approvals, inspections and field processes. When these workflows operate across disconnected systems, spreadsheets, emails and manual processes, project teams can face delays, fragmented information, limited visibility and unnecessary rework.

Our Solution

FieldOps Pro is being designed as a unified digital environment for construction field operations, connecting key workflows and project information across teams.

Permit Management

Coordinate permit requests, approvals and field status.

Safety & HSE

Support inspections, observations and hazard tracking.

RFI Management

Improve visibility across technical questions and responses.

Document Tracking

Connect project documents with field execution.

Meeting & Action Tracking

Capture decisions, responsibilities and follow-up actions.

Project Dashboards

Provide management-level visibility into field operations.

Cost & Document Analytics

Transform project information into actionable insight.

Rework Prevention

Identify operational signals that may contribute to preventable rework.
